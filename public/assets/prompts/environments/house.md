# ASSET GENERATION PROMPT: THE HOUSE THAT BREATHES (ENVIRONMENTS)

## Style & Cinematography
Cinematic practical horror film set, 1920s Pacific Northwest craftsman house, water damage, rotted cedar, peeling lead paint, dust motes drifting in cold air, rain streaming down exterior siding, warm muted flashlight beam cutting through thick charcoal shadows, authentic architectural decay without digital fantasy tropes.

## Scenes
1. **The Porch**: Weathered steps, front door ajar 10cm revealing pitch black interior, rain on cedar shingles.
2. **The Foyer**: Grandfather clock frozen at 03:17, dusty floorboards, dark doorway leading to parlor.
3. **The Parlor**: Hearth, dark mantelpiece with silver-framed family portrait from 1924.
4. **The Hallway**: Long claustrophobic corridor, peeling floral wallpaper, vanishing perspective.
5. **The Impossible Room**: Non-Euclidean geometry, no windows, mortar wall inscribed "YOU ARE NOT THE FIRST."
